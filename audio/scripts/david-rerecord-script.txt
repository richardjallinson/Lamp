1. Creation – opening
In the beginning, there was only darkness, and the deep… and the Spirit of God moving upon the face of the waters. Then God spoke. And everything began.

2. Creation – the verse
And God said, Let there be light: and there was light. Genesis, chapter 1, verse 3.

3. Creation – what happened next
Six days God worked, and everything he made was very good. On the seventh day, he rested. But in a garden eastward in Eden, a new story was about to begin.

4. Eden – opening
God formed man from the dust of the ground, and breathed into him the breath of life. Then he planted a garden eastward in Eden, full of every tree that is pleasant to the sight… and he set the man there, to keep it.

5. Eden – the verse
So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them. Genesis, chapter 1, verse 27.

6. Eden – what happened next
In the midst of the garden stood two trees: the tree of life, and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. Of that one tree, God said, thou shalt not eat. And somewhere in the garden… a serpent was watching.

7. The Four Rivers puzzle
And a river went out of Eden to water the garden; and from thence it was parted, and became into four heads. Genesis, chapter 2, verse 10.

8. The Fall – opening
The serpent was more subtle than any beast of the field. He spoke to the woman, and he twisted the words of God. She took of the fruit, and gave also to her husband… and the eyes of them both were opened.

9. The Fall – the verse
And the LORD God called unto Adam, and said unto him, Where art thou? Genesis, chapter 3, verse 9.

10. The Fall – what happened next
God made them coats of skins, and clothed them, and sent them out of the garden. At the east of Eden he placed cherubims, and a flaming sword which turned every way, to keep the way of the tree of life. The way was shut… but not forever.

11. The Garden Gate opens
Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city. Revelation, chapter 22, verse 14.
