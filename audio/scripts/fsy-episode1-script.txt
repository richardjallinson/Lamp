THE FINAL SEVEN YEARS – EPISODE 1: THE JERUSALEM MYSTERY

Record them in this order. No renaming needed – send them all together.

1. Opening
The Bible says a Temple will stand again in Jerusalem, in the last seven years. The vessels are ready. The priests are trained. The plans are drawn. Only one thing is missing: the place. For thirteen centuries the world has assumed it knew where the Temple stood. Come with me through Jerusalem, look at the evidence, and decide for yourself.

2. The Temple Institute – outside
Here the vessels of the Temple have been made again, ready for the day it stands: the Ark, the menorah, the harps and trumpets. Everything is prepared. Everything but the place. Go in, and look closely at five things.

3. The Ark – the verse
And the cherubims shall stretch forth their wings on high, covering the mercy seat with their wings, and their faces shall look one to another. Exodus, chapter 25, verse 20.

4. The Ark – the staves
The staves shall be in the rings of the ark: they shall not be taken from it. Exodus, chapter 25, verse 15.

5. Walk the Courts – the verse
Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise. Psalm 100, verse 4.

6. Walk the Courts – what it shows
From the Eastern Gate a pilgrim walked west, inward: the Court of the Women, a hundred and thirty-five cubits; then the Court of Israel, the Court of the Priests and the altar, another fifty-two. A hundred and eighty-seven cubits from the gate stood the sanctuary. Remember that number.

7. The instrument case – the verse
Praise him with the sound of the trumpet: praise him with the psaltery and harp. Psalm 150, verse 3.

7b. The instrument case – the Temple instruments
Every instrument in this case has been made for the Third Temple. The silver trumpets are the only instruments the Torah itself commands, blown by the priests over the offerings. The nevel and the kinnor, the harp and the lyre, are the instruments of the Levites. And the shofar is the ram's horn, the trumpet of the feast. Every one is waiting for a building.

7c. The Temple vessels
Every vessel of the Temple had a purpose. The menorah gave the Holy Place its only light. The golden altar carried the incense, morning and evening. The table held twelve loaves, one for every tribe. The brazen altar received the daily offering. And the High Priest wore a gold plate on his forehead: Holiness to the LORD. The vessels are ready. The place is the missing piece.

8. The parchment – the verse
And, behold, the glory of the God of Israel came from the way of the east: and his voice was like a noise of many waters: and the earth shined with his glory. Ezekiel, chapter 43, verse 2.

9. The parchment – what it shows
Ezekiel saw the glory of God enter the Temple from the east. The Temple faced east, toward the Mount of Olives. If you want to know where the Temple stood, stand on that mountain, and look back.

9b. Back at the entrance – the postcard
One card stands out. On the back, in blue ink: Stand where His feet shall stand, and look back at the gate. Zechariah 14:4. Then you will see where it stood.

10. The Mount of Olives
The postcard brought you here. And his feet shall stand in that day upon the mount of Olives, which is before Jerusalem on the east. Zechariah, chapter 14, verse 4. From here the Temple's east gate looked straight at you. Find the sealed gate in the city wall.

11. The Mount of Olives – the sealed gate
From the sanctuary, through the gate, to the Mount of Olives: one straight line. Hold that line in your mind. It is about to matter.

12. The Temple Mount – opening
The Bible says the Temple will stand where the first Temple stood. But where was that? For thirteen centuries the world has assumed it knew. Look at the evidence, and decide for yourself.

13. Before the clues
Before you look at the clues: where do you think the Holy of Holies stood?

14. The journal
Five clues are hidden in Scripture and history. Gather them, then decide.

15. Clue: the threshing floor
Then Solomon began to build the house of the LORD at Jerusalem in mount Moriah, in the place that David had prepared in the threshingfloor of Ornan the Jebusite. A threshing floor must be flat. Is the rock under the golden dome flat?

16. Clue: the top of the mountain
Josephus, a priest who served in the Temple, wrote that it stood on the very top of the mountain, on ground that needed no preparation.

17. Clue: the line from the Eastern Gate
The Temple faced east: from the sanctuary, through the Eastern Gate, to the Mount of Olives. Follow the line.

18. Clue: the measuring cubits
The Mishnah measures the courts from the Eastern Gate. Pace them out, and see where you stand.

19. Clue: the middle of the court
Moreover Solomon hallowed the middle of the court that was before the house of the LORD. The middle had to stay open. What stands there today?

20. The verdict
All five clues are in your journal. Where did the Holy of Holies stand?

21. The reveal
Rise, and measure the temple of God, and the altar, and them that worship therein. But the court which is without the temple leave out, and measure it not; for it is given unto the Gentiles. Revelation, chapter 11, verses 1 and 2.

22. The Eastern Gate – inside
You have walked the courts, stood on the mountain, and found the place. One gate is left: the one you saw from the Mount of Olives, from the other side. Ezekiel saw it shut, and wrote down why. Fill in his words.

23. The Eastern Gate – the verse
Then said the LORD unto me; This gate shall be shut, it shall not be opened, and no man shall enter in by it; because the LORD, the God of Israel, hath entered in by it, therefore it shall be shut. It is for the prince. Ezekiel, chapter 44, verses 2 and 3.

24. The cliffhanger
The Temple can stand. The gate is shut, waiting for the Prince. But before the King comes, another will come first, and confirm a covenant with many. Sixty-nine of Daniel's seventy weeks are fulfilled. One week remains.
