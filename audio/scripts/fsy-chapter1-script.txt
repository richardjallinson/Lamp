THE FINAL SEVEN YEARS – CHAPTER 1: WHERE WAS THE TEMPLE?
Lines for the Lamplighter (Oliver Silk), to record later in ElevenLabs.
Record them in this order. No renaming needed.

1. Opening
The Bible says a Temple will stand again in Jerusalem, in the last seven years. It will stand where the first Temple stood. But where was that? For thirteen centuries the world has assumed it knew. Look at the evidence, and decide for yourself.

2. Before the clues
Before you look at the clues: where do you think the Holy of Holies stood?

3. The journal
Five clues are hidden in Scripture and history. Gather them, then decide.

4. Clue: the threshing floor
Then Solomon began to build the house of the LORD at Jerusalem in mount Moriah, in the place that David had prepared in the threshingfloor of Ornan the Jebusite. A threshing floor must be flat. Is the rock under the golden dome flat?

5. Clue: the top of the mountain
Josephus, a priest who served in the Temple, wrote that it stood on the very top of the mountain, on ground that needed no preparation.

6. Clue: the line from the Eastern Gate
The Temple faced east: from the sanctuary, through the Eastern Gate, to the Mount of Olives. Follow the line.

7. Clue: the measuring cubits
The Mishnah measures the courts from the Eastern Gate. Pace them out, and see where you stand.

8. Clue: the middle of the court
Moreover Solomon hallowed the middle of the court that was before the house of the LORD. The middle had to stay open. What stands there today?

9. The verdict
All five clues are in your journal. Where did the Holy of Holies stand?

10. The reveal
Rise, and measure the temple of God, and the altar, and them that worship therein. But the court which is without the temple leave out, and measure it not; for it is given unto the Gentiles.

11. Coming next
The Temple can stand. But when? Sixty-nine of Daniel's seventy weeks are fulfilled. One week remains.
