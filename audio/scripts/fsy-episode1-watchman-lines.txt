THE FINAL SEVEN YEARS – EPISODE 1: THE WATCHMAN LINES
New lines for the Watchman story. Record in this order, send un-renamed.

HANK (same voice and settings as before)

1b. The luggage
Your bag comes round the carousel. Tucked under the strap is a folded card that was not there when you packed. No name. Only a verse, in blue ink, and a small drawn lamp. I have set watchmen upon thy walls, O Jerusalem, which shall never hold their peace day nor night. Isaiah, chapter 62, verse 6.

10b. The Tombs of the Prophets
Haggai. Zechariah. Malachi. The last voices of the Old Testament lie here, under the Mount of Olives. And someone is down here ahead of you. Fresh footprints in the dust… and a light at the far end that has just gone out.

10c. The oil
Not by might, nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the LORD of hosts. Zechariah's lampstand, fed by two olive trees, here in Zechariah's own tomb. Fill your lamp.

10d. The line of flax
A line of flax, and a measuring reed, in the hand of a man who stood in the gate. Ezekiel, chapter 40, verse 3. Someone has given you the means to measure the Temple. And a warning: He is looking too. Go quickly.

22b. The Watchman's last note
You found the place. Now so has he. Those he serves will put it on the table. Read Daniel, chapter 9, verse 27.

THE MAN IN THE GREY COAT (a second, different voice: calm, polite, cold)

grey-1
So you say the Temple stood up here, at that little dome? Most people would point at the golden one. Give me your three best reasons.

grey-2
Thank you. That's all I needed.
