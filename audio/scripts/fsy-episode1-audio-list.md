# The Jerusalem Mystery: audio to make

Episode 1 of *The Final Seven Years* in Lamp.

The plan follows Myst and Riven. Quiet location sounds play underneath at all times. Music changes with each place. Hank speaks at the important moments. Silence is allowed.

**How to send:** make each group in the order listed and send them un-renamed, one group at a time. I match files by their time stamps. You don't need to type any names.

## How it will play

- Each place has its background sound looping softly underneath.
- The music fades from one place to the next. During a puzzle it changes to the calm puzzle track.
- When Hank speaks, the music dips so his voice stays clear.
- Settings will get separate on/off switches for music and for sounds.
- Anything not made yet simply stays silent, so files can arrive one group at a time.
