# LAMP story list — New Testament (KJV). Same format as stories-ot.txt.

== magi | We Have Seen His Star
draw: a bright star over the hills of Bethlehem at night, camels and travellers seen as distant silhouettes on the road below
Matthew 2:2 | Saying, Where is he that is born King of the Jews? for we have seen his star in the east, and are come to worship him.
* Matthew 2:10 | When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceeding great joy.
Matthew 2:11 | And when they were come into the house, they saw the young child with Mary his mother, and fell down, and worshipped him: and when they had opened their treasures, they presented unto him gifts; gold, and frankincense, and myrrh.
note: The wise men came from the east, perhaps Babylon or Persia, where Daniel had once served. They found the child in a house, not the stable, possibly up to two years later (2:16).

== follow | Follow Me
draw: fishing boats pulled up on the shore of the Sea of Galilee at dawn, nets drying on the rocks, mist on the water
Matthew 4:4 | But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.
* Matthew 4:19 | And he saith unto them, Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men.
note: Jesus answered each temptation with "It is written," quoting Deuteronomy each time. Then he called ordinary fishermen to follow him.

== blessed | The Beatitudes
draw: a grassy hillside above the Sea of Galilee in soft afternoon light, wildflowers, a crowd seated far off seen from behind
Matthew 5:3 | Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.
* Matthew 5:8 | Blessed are the pure in heart: for they shall see God.
Matthew 5:9 | Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.
note: The Sermon on the Mount opens with eight blessings that turn the world's values upside down. It begins with the poor in spirit, those who know they need God.

== light | The Light of the World
draw: a city of white stone set on a hill at night, its windows glowing, and in the foreground an oil lamp on a stand inside a doorway
* Matthew 5:14 | Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid.
Matthew 5:15 | Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house.
Matthew 5:16 | Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.
note: Jesus said, "I am the light of the world" (John 8:12), and told his followers that they are too. A lamp is lit to be seen.

== prayer | The Lord's Prayer
draw: a quiet olive grove at first light, a simple room's open window looking out on it, a loaf of bread on the sill
* Matthew 6:9 | After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name.
Matthew 6:10 | Thy kingdom come. Thy will be done in earth, as it is in heaven.
Matthew 6:11 | Give us this day our daily bread.
Matthew 6:12 | And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors.
Matthew 6:13 | And lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil: For thine is the kingdom, and the power, and the glory, for ever. Amen.
note: Jesus gave this prayer as a pattern: "After this manner." It puts God's name, kingdom and will first, and then our daily needs.

== seek | Seek Ye First
draw: birds flying over a field of wild lilies in bloom, a barn far off, warm afternoon light
Matthew 6:26 | Behold the fowls of the air: for they sow not, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feedeth them. Are ye not much better than they?
* Matthew 6:33 | But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.
Matthew 7:7 | Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you:
note: Worry looks at tomorrow; faith looks at the Father who feeds the birds. Put his kingdom first and he takes care of the rest.

== rest | Come unto Me
draw: a wooden yoke resting on a stone wall beside a quiet field at sunset, oxen grazing freely nearby
* Matthew 11:28 | Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.
Matthew 11:29 | Take my yoke upon you, and learn of me; for I am meek and lowly in heart: and ye shall find rest unto your souls.
Matthew 11:30 | For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.
note: A yoke joined two animals to pull together. Jesus offers to share the load, and calls his yoke "easy," meaning well-fitting.

== water | Be of Good Cheer
draw: a fishing boat tossed on a stormy lake at night, and far across the waves a single figure walking on the water, seen only as a silhouette in moonlight
* Matthew 14:27 | But straightway Jesus spake unto them, saying, Be of good cheer; it is I; be not afraid.
Matthew 14:29 | And he said, Come. And when Peter was come down out of the ship, he walked on the water, to go to Jesus.
Matthew 14:31 | And immediately Jesus stretched forth his hand, and caught him, and said unto him, O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?
note: Peter walked on water while he looked at Jesus and began to sink when he looked at the wind. Even then, Jesus caught him immediately.

== signs | My Words Shall Not Pass Away
draw: a fig tree putting out new leaves on the Mount of Olives at dusk, the temple mount across the valley, storm light on the horizon
Matthew 24:14 | And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations; and then shall the end come.
* Matthew 24:35 | Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away.
Matthew 24:36 | But of that day and hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels of heaven, but my Father only.
note: On the Mount of Olives Jesus told his disciples about the signs before his return. He said to learn the lesson of the fig tree and to watch, because no one knows the day or the hour.

== go | Go Ye Therefore
draw: an empty garden tomb at sunrise with the great stone rolled away, linen cloths folded inside, lilies by the path
Matthew 28:6 | He is not here: for he is risen, as he said. Come, see the place where the Lord lay.
* Matthew 28:19 | Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:
Matthew 28:20 | Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.
note: The risen Christ sent his followers to all nations with a promise: "I am with you alway." Matthew's Gospel ends with that promise.

== sower | Behold, a Sower
draw: a field being sown at morning, seed scattered over a path, rocks, thorns and good dark soil, birds overhead
* Mark 4:3 | Hearken; Behold, there went out a sower to sow:
Mark 4:20 | And these are they which are sown on good ground; such as hear the word, and receive it, and bring forth fruit, some thirtyfold, some sixty, and some an hundred.
note: Jesus explained this parable himself: the seed is the word. The same seed fell on every kind of ground; the difference was the soil.

== calm | Peace, Be Still
draw: the Sea of Galilee suddenly calm after a storm, clouds breaking, moonlight on glassy water, a small boat resting
* Mark 4:39 | And he arose, and rebuked the wind, and said unto the sea, Peace, be still. And the wind ceased, and there was a great calm.
Mark 4:41 | And they feared exceedingly, and said one to another, What manner of man is this, that even the wind and the sea obey him?
note: Only God rules the sea (Psalm 89:9). The disciples' question, "What manner of man is this?", is the question of Mark's whole Gospel.

== possible | All Things Are Possible
draw: a narrow mountain pass opening onto a bright valley, the path ahead lit through the gap in the rocks
* Mark 9:23 | Jesus said unto him, If thou canst believe, all things are possible to him that believeth.
Mark 9:24 | And straightway the father of the child cried out, and said with tears, Lord, I believe; help thou mine unbelief.
Mark 10:27 | And Jesus looking upon them saith, With men it is impossible, but not with God: for with God all things are possible.
note: The father's honest prayer, "Lord, I believe; help thou mine unbelief," was enough. Jesus healed his son.

== magnificat | With God Nothing Shall Be Impossible
draw: a simple stone house in Nazareth at dawn, a lily in a clay jar on the windowsill, light streaming into the room
* Luke 1:37 | For with God nothing shall be impossible.
Luke 1:38 | And Mary said, Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it unto me according to thy word. And the angel departed from her.
Luke 1:46 | And Mary said, My soul doth magnify the Lord,
Luke 1:47 | And my spirit hath rejoiced in God my Saviour.
note: Mary answered the impossible with trust: "be it unto me according to thy word." Her song, the Magnificat, is full of echoes of Hannah's prayer in 1 Samuel 2.

== tidings | Good Tidings of Great Joy
draw: shepherds' fields outside Bethlehem at night, sheep resting, the sky opening with soft golden light, the town small on the hill
Luke 2:7 | And she brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him in swaddling clothes, and laid him in a manger; because there was no room for them in the inn.
Luke 2:10 | And the angel said unto them, Fear not: for, behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy, which shall be to all people.
* Luke 2:11 | For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord.
Luke 2:14 | Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men.
note: The first announcement of Christ's birth went to shepherds in the fields near Bethlehem, the city of David the shepherd king.

== samaritan | Go, and Do Thou Likewise
draw: the steep rocky road from Jerusalem down to Jericho in late afternoon, a donkey tied outside a lit roadside inn
Luke 10:27 | And he answering said, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy strength, and with all thy mind; and thy neighbour as thyself.
Luke 10:33 | But a certain Samaritan, as he journeyed, came where he was: and when he saw him, he had compassion on him,
* Luke 10:37 | And he said, He that shewed mercy on him. Then said Jesus unto him, Go, and do thou likewise.
note: A lawyer asked, "Who is my neighbour?" Jesus turned the question around: the neighbour was the one who showed mercy, and he was a Samaritan.

== lost | Lost and Found
draw: a shepherd seen from behind carrying a lamb on his shoulders down a hillside at dusk, a village lamp glowing below
Luke 15:4 | What man of you, having an hundred sheep, if he lose one of them, doth not leave the ninety and nine in the wilderness, and go after that which is lost, until he find it?
Luke 15:20 | And he arose, and came to his father. But when he was yet a great way off, his father saw him, and had compassion, and ran, and fell on his neck, and kissed him.
* Luke 15:24 | For this my son was dead, and is alive again; he was lost, and is found. And they began to be merry.
note: Luke 15 has three parables of lost things: a sheep, a coin and a son. Each ends in rejoicing, and the father ran to meet his son.

== paradise | Remember Me
draw: three empty crosses on a distant hill under a darkened sky, a thin line of light breaking on the horizon
Luke 23:34 | Then said Jesus, Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do. And they parted his raiment, and cast lots.
Luke 23:42 | And he said unto Jesus, Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom.
* Luke 23:43 | And Jesus said unto him, Verily I say unto thee, To day shalt thou be with me in paradise.
note: Even on the cross Jesus prayed for those who crucified him. A dying thief asked to be remembered and was promised paradise that same day.

== emmaus | Did Not Our Heart Burn?
draw: a dusty road at sunset winding toward a small village, three travellers seen from far behind, a lamp lit in a doorway ahead
Luke 24:27 | And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself.
* Luke 24:32 | And they said one to another, Did not our heart burn within us, while he talked with us by the way, and while he opened to us the scriptures?
note: The risen Jesus taught from Moses and all the prophets about himself. The whole Old Testament points to him.

== word | In the Beginning Was the Word
draw: a single beam of golden light shining into deep darkness over the waters, echoing the first painting of Creation
* John 1:1 | In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.
John 1:5 | And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.
John 1:14 | And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth.
note: John begins with the same words as Genesis: "In the beginning." "Dwelt among us" literally means "pitched his tent among us," like the tabernacle.

== loved | For God So Loved the World
draw: the river Jordan at Bethany beyond Jordan at sunrise, reeds and still water, a lamb standing on the bank
John 1:29 | The next day John seeth Jesus coming unto him, and saith, Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world.
* John 3:16 | For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.
John 3:17 | For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; but that the world through him might be saved.
note: John 3:16 may be the best-known verse in the Bible. Jesus spoke it to Nicodemus, a teacher of Israel who came to him by night.

== thirst | Never Thirst
draw: an old stone well in Samaria at noon, a clay water jar left beside it, Mount Gerizim behind, a basket of five loaves and two fish on the grass nearby
John 4:14 | But whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst; but the water that I shall give him shall be in him a well of water springing up into everlasting life.
John 4:24 | God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth.
* John 6:35 | And Jesus said unto them, I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger; and he that believeth on me shall never thirst.
note: Jesus offered living water to a Samaritan woman, and called himself the bread of life after feeding five thousand. "I am the bread of life" is the first of his seven "I am" sayings in John.

== lightlife | The Truth Shall Make You Free
draw: the temple courts in Jerusalem at night during the Feast of Tabernacles, giant golden lampstands blazing, the city glowing below
* John 8:12 | Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.
John 8:32 | And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.
note: At the Feast of Tabernacles great lamps were lit in the temple courts. In that setting Jesus said, "I am the light of the world."

== goodshepherd | The Good Shepherd
draw: a stone sheepfold on a hillside at evening, sheep entering through the single gate, a shepherd seen from behind standing in the doorway
John 10:10 | The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly.
* John 10:11 | I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep.
John 10:27 | My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me:
note: Shepherds in Israel often slept across the doorway of the fold. The Good Shepherd does more: he lays down his life for the sheep.

== lazarus | The Resurrection and the Life
draw: a rock tomb at Bethany with its stone rolled away, light spilling out of the dark doorway, olive trees around
* John 11:25 | Jesus said unto her, I am the resurrection, and the life: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live:
John 11:35 | Jesus wept.
John 11:43 | And when he thus had spoken, he cried with a loud voice, Lazarus, come forth.
note: "Jesus wept" is the shortest verse in the KJV. He wept at the grave even though he was about to raise Lazarus from the dead.

== way | The Way, the Truth, and the Life
draw: a lamp-lit stone house with many rooms on a hill, doors open and light pouring out onto a single path leading up to it
John 14:1 | Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me.
John 14:2 | In my Father's house are many mansions: if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you.
* John 14:6 | Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.
note: Jesus said these words at the Last Supper, the night before the cross. "Mansions" means dwelling places in the Father's house.

== believe | That Ye Might Believe
draw: the shore of the Sea of Galilee at dawn, a small charcoal fire with fish laid on it, a boat drawn up and nets full of fish
John 19:30 | When Jesus therefore had received the vinegar, he said, It is finished: and he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost.
John 20:29 | Jesus saith unto him, Thomas, because thou hast seen me, thou hast believed: blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed.
* John 20:31 | But these are written, that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing ye might have life through his name.
note: "It is finished" is one word in Greek, tetelestai, used for a debt paid in full. John tells us exactly why he wrote his Gospel: that we might believe.

== power | Ye Shall Receive Power
draw: the top of the Mount of Olives with a cloud of light rising into the sky, Jerusalem below, onlookers seen from behind
* Acts 1:8 | But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judaea, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth.
Acts 1:11 | Which also said, Ye men of Galilee, why stand ye gazing up into heaven? this same Jesus, which is taken up from you into heaven, shall so come in like manner as ye have seen him go into heaven.
note: Acts 1:8 is an outline of the whole book: Jerusalem, Judaea and Samaria, then the ends of the earth. The angels promised he would return "in like manner."

== pentecost | Whosoever Shall Call
draw: an upper room at morning with a rushing wind blowing curtains, small flames of light hovering in the air, the city beyond the window
Acts 2:3 | And there appeared unto them cloven tongues like as of fire, and it sat upon each of them.
* Acts 2:21 | And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be saved.
Acts 4:12 | Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.
note: Pentecost, the Feast of Weeks, came fifty days after Passover. Peter preached from Joel 2, and about three thousand people believed that day.

== damascus | The Road to Damascus
draw: a desert road near Damascus at noon, a blinding light bursting from the sky, a fallen traveller's cloak and staff in the dust
Acts 9:3 | And as he journeyed, he came near Damascus: and suddenly there shined round about him a light from heaven:
* Acts 9:4 | And he fell to the earth, and heard a voice saying unto him, Saul, Saul, why persecutest thou me?
Acts 9:18 | And immediately there fell from his eyes as it had been scales: and he received sight forthwith, and arose, and was baptized.
note: Saul hunted Christians until the risen Christ met him on the road. Persecuting the church was persecuting Christ himself. Saul became Paul, who wrote thirteen New Testament letters.

== bereans | They Searched the Scriptures
draw: a synagogue room in Berea lit by oil lamps, scrolls unrolled on a long table, a window open to the evening
Acts 16:31 | And they said, Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and thou shalt be saved, and thy house.
* Acts 17:11 | These were more noble than those in Thessalonica, in that they received the word with all readiness of mind, and searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so.
Acts 17:28 | For in him we live, and move, and have our being; as certain also of your own poets have said, For we are also his offspring.
note: The Bereans checked even an apostle's teaching against Scripture, and Luke calls them noble for it. Paul quoted Greek poets in Athens to meet his hearers where they were.

== sinned | Not Ashamed of the Gospel
draw: the city of Rome at dawn, seen from a hill, the road leading in and the river shining, a scroll letter sealed in the foreground
Romans 1:16 | For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.
* Romans 3:23 | For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;
note: Romans is Paul's fullest explanation of the gospel. Before the good news comes the bad news: no one measures up.

== gift | The Gift of God
draw: a simple wrapped gift resting on a stone table in a ray of light, an open doorway behind leading out into a sunlit garden
Romans 5:8 | But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.
* Romans 6:23 | For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.
Romans 10:17 | So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.
note: Wages are earned and a gift is received. God showed his love while we were still sinners, not after we improved.

== together | All Things Work Together
draw: a wide river delta seen from above at sunset, many streams winding together into one great water flowing to the sea
* Romans 8:28 | And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.
Romans 8:38 | For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come,
Romans 8:39 | Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.
note: Romans 8 begins with "no condemnation" and ends with "no separation." Nothing in all creation can separate us from God's love.

== sacrifice | A Living Sacrifice
draw: a stone altar in a quiet courtyard with a small fire, morning light, the city waking beyond the wall
Romans 12:1 | I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.
* Romans 12:2 | And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.
Romans 12:21 | Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good.
note: After eleven chapters of what God has done, Paul writes "therefore." Living for God is the response to his mercy, not the way to earn it.

== charity | The Greatest of These
draw: an old bronze mirror on a table reflecting a soft blurred light from a window, three lit oil lamps beside it
1 Corinthians 13:4 | Charity suffereth long, and is kind; charity envieth not; charity vaunteth not itself, is not puffed up,
1 Corinthians 13:12 | For now we see through a glass, darkly; but then face to face: now I know in part; but then shall I know even as also I am known.
* 1 Corinthians 13:13 | And now abideth faith, hope, charity, these three; but the greatest of these is charity.
note: "Charity" in the KJV translates agape, self-giving love. A "glass" here is a polished metal mirror, which gave only a dim reflection.

== twinkling | In the Twinkling of an Eye
draw: a vast sky at dawn with clouds parting and golden light streaming down over a quiet graveyard of old stones, trumpet-like rays of light
1 Corinthians 15:52 | In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trump: for the trumpet shall sound, and the dead shall be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed.
* 1 Corinthians 15:55 | O death, where is thy sting? O grave, where is thy victory?
1 Corinthians 15:57 | But thanks be to God, which giveth us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.
note: 1 Corinthians 15 is the Bible's great resurrection chapter. Paul lists more than five hundred eyewitnesses of the risen Christ (15:6).

== newcreature | A New Creature
draw: a butterfly drying its wings on a twig beside an empty chrysalis, spring meadow behind in morning light
2 Corinthians 5:7 | (For we walk by faith, not by sight:)
* 2 Corinthians 5:17 | Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.
2 Corinthians 12:9 | And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.
note: Paul asked three times for his "thorn in the flesh" to be removed. God's answer was not removal but grace.

== fruit | The Fruit of the Spirit
draw: a vine and a fruit tree heavy with ripe fruit in a walled garden, grapes, figs and pomegranates in warm light
Galatians 2:20 | I am crucified with Christ: nevertheless I live; yet not I, but Christ liveth in me: and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by the faith of the Son of God, who loved me, and gave himself for me.
* Galatians 5:22 | But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith,
Galatians 5:23 | Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.
Galatians 6:9 | And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.
note: Paul says "fruit," not "fruits": one harvest with nine qualities, grown by the Spirit rather than manufactured by effort.

== grace | By Grace Are Ye Saved
draw: a craftsman's workshop with a finished, beautiful carved piece on the bench, tools laid down, light from a high window
* Ephesians 2:8 | For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God:
Ephesians 2:9 | Not of works, lest any man should boast.
Ephesians 2:10 | For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.
note: Salvation is not by works, but it is for good works. "Workmanship" is the Greek poiema, the root of our word "poem."

== armour | The Whole Armour of God
draw: a Roman soldier's armour laid out on a wooden stand: helmet, shield, belt, breastplate and sword, in lamplight
Ephesians 6:10 | Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might.
* Ephesians 6:11 | Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.
Ephesians 6:17 | And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God:
note: Paul wrote from prison, likely chained to a Roman soldier. The only weapon for attack in the armour is the sword of the Spirit, the word of God.

== allthings | I Can Do All Things
draw: a prison cell window with sunlight streaming in, a letter and quill on a small table, chains lying loose on the floor
Philippians 4:6 | Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God.
Philippians 4:7 | And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.
* Philippians 4:13 | I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.
note: Paul wrote "rejoice" again and again from prison. In context, "all things" means being content in plenty and in want (4:11-12).

== above | Set Your Affection on Things Above
draw: a hilltop above the clouds at dawn, the sky deep blue overhead with the morning star still shining
Colossians 1:17 | And he is before all things, and by him all things consist.
* Colossians 3:2 | Set your affection on things above, not on things on the earth.
Colossians 3:23 | And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men;
note: Christ holds all things together. Because of that, ordinary work becomes service done "as to the Lord."

== caughtup | Caught Up Together
draw: a sky full of bright parting clouds over a sleeping town at dawn, rays of light streaming downward and upward
1 Thessalonians 4:16 | For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in Christ shall rise first:
* 1 Thessalonians 4:17 | Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air: and so shall we ever be with the Lord.
1 Thessalonians 5:18 | In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.
note: Paul wrote this to comfort believers grieving those who had died: "Wherefore comfort one another with these words" (4:18).

== standfast | Stand Fast
draw: a lighthouse-like watchtower standing firm on a rocky coast as waves crash below, its lamp burning
2 Thessalonians 2:15 | Therefore, brethren, stand fast, and hold the traditions which ye have been taught, whether by word, or our epistle.
* 2 Thessalonians 3:3 | But the Lord is faithful, who shall stablish you, and keep you from evil.
2 Thessalonians 3:13 | But ye, brethren, be not weary in well doing.
note: The church in Thessalonica had been unsettled by false reports about the day of the Lord. Paul told them to stand fast in what they had been taught.

== example | Godliness with Contentment
draw: a simple table with bread, a cup and an open scroll by a window, a quiet garden outside in soft light
1 Timothy 2:5 | For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus;
1 Timothy 4:12 | Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.
* 1 Timothy 6:6 | But godliness with contentment is great gain.
note: Paul wrote to Timothy, a young pastor in Ephesus. "Conversation" in the KJV means way of life, not just speech.

== study | Rightly Dividing the Word
draw: a worn scroll and a carpenter's straightedge on a workbench, a lamp and a window looking out on a long road at sunset
2 Timothy 2:15 | Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.
2 Timothy 3:16 | All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness:
* 2 Timothy 4:7 | I have fought a good fight, I have finished my course, I have kept the faith:
note: 2 Timothy is Paul's last letter, written shortly before his death. "Inspiration of God" is literally "God-breathed."

== titus | That Blessed Hope
draw: the island of Crete at sunrise, cliffs over a sparkling sea, a small harbour town waking
* Titus 2:13 | Looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ;
Titus 3:5 | Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy he saved us, by the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost;
note: Titus was left in Crete to set churches in order. Paul ties right living to looking forward to Christ's appearing.

== philemon | A Brother Beloved
draw: the open door of a house in Colossae at evening, a lamp in the window, a traveller's bag and staff set down at the threshold
* Philemon 1:6 | That the communication of thy faith may become effectual by the acknowledging of every good thing which is in you in Christ Jesus.
Philemon 1:16 | Not now as a servant, but above a servant, a brother beloved, specially to me, but how much more unto thee, both in the flesh, and in the Lord?
note: Paul sent Onesimus, a runaway servant who had become a Christian, back to his master Philemon, asking that he be welcomed as a brother.

== quick | Quick and Powerful
draw: a two-edged sword lying across an open scroll on a stone, a beam of light falling on both, deep shadows around
Hebrews 4:12 | For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.
Hebrews 4:16 | Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need.
* Hebrews 13:8 | Jesus Christ the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever.
note: "Quick" in the KJV means living. God's word is alive and searches the heart, and Christ never changes.

== faith | Now Faith Is
draw: an ancient road leading toward a distant city of light on the horizon, tents and altars of travellers along the way, a starry dawn sky
* Hebrews 11:1 | Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.
Hebrews 11:6 | But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.
Hebrews 12:1 | Wherefore seeing we also are compassed about with so great a cloud of witnesses, let us lay aside every weight, and the sin which doth so easily beset us, and let us run with patience the race that is set before us,
note: Hebrews 11 is the hall of faith, from Abel to the prophets. Those witnesses surround us as we run our own race.

== doers | Doers of the Word
draw: a clear still pool reflecting a person's silhouette from behind, a path leading away from it into a busy village
James 1:5 | If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.
* James 1:22 | But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.
James 4:8 | Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you. Cleanse your hands, ye sinners; and purify your hearts, ye double minded.
note: James, the brother of the Lord, wrote a practical letter. Hearing without doing, he says, is like looking in a mirror and forgetting your own face (1:23-24).

== care | He Careth for You
draw: a fisherman's net and a heavy pack laid down on a lakeshore at sunset, the water calm and gold
1 Peter 2:9 | But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light:
1 Peter 3:15 | But sanctify the Lord God in your hearts: and be ready always to give an answer to every man that asketh you a reason of the hope that is in you with meekness and fear:
* 1 Peter 5:7 | Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.
note: Peter wrote to scattered believers facing hardship. "Peculiar" in the KJV means God's own special possession.

== daystar | A Light That Shineth in a Dark Place
draw: a lamp burning on a rock in a dark landscape just before dawn, the morning star bright above the horizon
2 Peter 1:19 | We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place, until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts:
* 2 Peter 3:8 | But, beloved, be not ignorant of this one thing, that one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.
2 Peter 3:9 | The Lord is not slack concerning his promise, as some men count slackness; but is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.
note: Peter, an eyewitness of the transfiguration, still called the prophetic word "more sure," a lamp in a dark place. God's delay is patience, not slowness.

== godislight | God Is Light
draw: sunlight breaking through a dark forest into a clearing, every shadow lit, a stream running clear
1 John 1:5 | This then is the message which we have heard of him, and declare unto you, that God is light, and in him is no darkness at all.
* 1 John 1:9 | If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.
note: John wrote so that believers could know they have eternal life (5:13). Forgiveness rests on God's faithfulness, not on how we feel.

== godislove | God Is Love
draw: a warm hearth fire in a simple home at evening, empty chairs drawn close, a lamp on the table
1 John 4:8 | He that loveth not knoweth not God; for God is love.
* 1 John 4:19 | We love him, because he first loved us.
note: "God is love" appears only in 1 John 4 (verses 8 and 16). Our love is always a response; he loved first.

== twojohn | Walk After His Commandments
draw: a garden path between flowering hedges at morning, footprints leading forward, a house with an open door at the end
2 John 1:5 | And now I beseech thee, lady, not as though I wrote a new commandment unto thee, but that which we had from the beginning, that we love one another.
* 2 John 1:6 | And this is love, that we walk after his commandments. This is the commandment, That, as ye have heard from the beginning, ye should walk in it.
note: 2 John is written to "the elect lady and her children," probably a church family. Love and truth go together.

== threejohn | Walk in Truth
draw: a road through green hills with a small group of travellers seen from far behind, a welcoming lit house waiting for them
3 John 1:2 | Beloved, I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health, even as thy soul prospereth.
* 3 John 1:4 | I have no greater joy than to hear that my children walk in truth.
3 John 1:11 | Beloved, follow not that which is evil, but that which is good. He that doeth good is of God: but he that doeth evil hath not seen God.
note: 3 John is the shortest book in the Bible by word count in the original Greek. It thanks Gaius for his hospitality to travelling teachers.

== jude | Able to Keep You from Falling
draw: a narrow mountain path along a cliff edge in golden light, a strong rope railing guiding the way to the summit
* Jude 1:24 | Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy,
Jude 1:25 | To the only wise God our Saviour, be glory and majesty, dominion and power, both now and ever. Amen.
note: Jude, another brother of the Lord, wrote a sharp warning against false teachers, then ended with one of the Bible's most beautiful doxologies.

== alpha | Alpha and Omega
draw: seven golden lampstands burning in a dark space, their light reflecting on a sea of glass, stars above
Revelation 1:3 | Blessed is he that readeth, and they that hear the words of this prophecy, and keep those things which are written therein: for the time is at hand.
* Revelation 1:8 | I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the ending, saith the Lord, which is, and which was, and which is to come, the Almighty.
Revelation 1:12 | And I turned to see the voice that spake with me. And being turned, I saw seven golden candlesticks;
note: Revelation is the only book with a blessing for those who read it aloud and those who hear and keep it. The seven lampstands are the seven churches (1:20).

== door | Behold, I Stand at the Door
draw: an old wooden door with iron hinges and no outside handle, a lantern hanging beside it, evening light, ivy around the frame
Revelation 3:11 | Behold, I come quickly: hold that fast which thou hast, that no man take thy crown.
* Revelation 3:20 | Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me.
note: This was spoken to Laodicea, a church that thought it needed nothing. Christ stands outside and knocks, and the door is opened from within.

== worthy | Worthy Is the Lamb
draw: a vast throne room of light, a rainbow like emerald circling a throne shown only as radiance, a sea of glass like crystal before it
* Revelation 4:11 | Thou art worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honour and power: for thou hast created all things, and for thy pleasure they are and were created.
Revelation 5:12 | Saying with a loud voice, Worthy is the Lamb that was slain to receive power, and riches, and wisdom, and strength, and honour, and glory, and blessing.
note: In chapter 4 heaven worships God as Creator; in chapter 5, the Lamb as Redeemer. John looked for a lion and saw a Lamb (5:5-6).

== reign | He Shall Reign for Ever
draw: a white horse in the opened heavens among blazing clouds, seen from a distance with no rider's face, light pouring over the earth below
Revelation 11:15 | And the seventh angel sounded; and there were great voices in heaven, saying, The kingdoms of this world are become the kingdoms of our Lord, and of his Christ; and he shall reign for ever and ever.
Revelation 19:6 | And I heard as it were the voice of a great multitude, and as the voice of many waters, and as the voice of mighty thunderings, saying, Alleluia: for the Lord God omnipotent reigneth.
* Revelation 19:16 | And he hath on his vesture and on his thigh a name written, KING OF KINGS, AND LORD OF LORDS.
note: The kingdoms of this world become his kingdom. Handel set these words in the "Hallelujah" chorus of Messiah.

== newall | All Things New
draw: a golden city descending out of heaven, walls of jasper and gates of pearl shining, no sea, a new earth green below
Revelation 21:1 | And I saw a new heaven and a new earth: for the first heaven and the first earth were passed away; and there was no more sea.
Revelation 21:4 | And God shall wipe away all tears from their eyes; and there shall be no more death, neither sorrow, nor crying, neither shall there be any more pain: for the former things are passed away.
* Revelation 21:5 | And he that sat upon the throne said, Behold, I make all things new. And he said unto me, Write: for these words are true and faithful.
note: The Bible that began with a garden ends with a city. No more death, sorrow, crying or pain.

== come | Even So, Come
draw: a crystal river flowing from a city of light, the tree of life on both banks heavy with fruit, no sun or lamp, everything glowing from within
Revelation 22:5 | And there shall be no night there; and they need no candle, neither light of the sun; for the Lord God giveth them light: and they shall reign for ever and ever.
Revelation 22:17 | And the Spirit and the bride say, Come. And let him that heareth say, Come. And let him that is athirst come. And whosoever will, let him take the water of life freely.
* Revelation 22:20 | He which testifieth these things saith, Surely I come quickly. Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus.
note: The story that began with "Let there be light" ends where no lamp is needed, because the Lord God gives the light. The last prayer of the Bible is "Even so, come, Lord Jesus."
