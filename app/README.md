# Lamp 1.0 app

The game is a web app (`www/`) shown full screen inside a small SwiftUI shell, the same way as Weekly Table.

```
app/
  Lamp.xcodeproj        Xcode project (generated by tools/make_xcodeproj.py)
  Lamp/                 Swift shell, icon, entitlements, privacy manifest, StoreKit test file
  www/                  the game: index.html, css/, js/, img/
  tools/build_www.py    copies levels and finished pictures into www/
  tools/make_sounds.py  makes every sound from scratch and packs them into www/js/sounds.js
  sounds/               the sounds as mp3 (the .wav masters are rebuilt by make_sounds.py)
  tools/make_xcodeproj.py  rewrites the project file after adding Swift files
  tools/make_intro_assets.py  makes the start screen pictures in www/img/start/
  tools/make_title_music.py   makes the start screen music (sounds/title*.mp3, www/js/intro-sounds.js)
  tools/make_adventure_assets.py  makes the adventure pictures in www/img/adv/
```

## What the shell does

| Message from the game | What iOS does |
|---|---|
| `ready` | Sends back the saved progress (this iPhone's or iCloud's, whichever is newer), the price and whether the full Bible is unlocked |
| `save` | Keeps a copy of progress in UserDefaults and in the user's own iCloud (key-value storage) |
| `purchase` / `restore` | StoreKit 2 purchase or restore of `com.richardallinson.Lamp.fullbible` |
| `haptic` | Light tap, success or warning vibration |
| `share` | The iOS share sheet with a verse or daily result |
| `review` | The App Store rating prompt (asked once, after the third story) |

## Start screen

Every time the app opens, the start screen (`www/js/intro.js`, `www/css/intro.css`) shows the clay lamp
in the dark. Tap it and it lights; the lamp flies up the golden road of the New Jerusalem painting,
behind the painted LAMP letters and through the gate on the music's hit, then "Begin the journey"
(or "Continue the journey") opens the game.

- **First time:** the full version, about 9 seconds, with Psalm 119:105 held on screen for three seconds.
- **After that:** a quick version, about 2 seconds, with its own short music cue.
- **Skip** goes straight to the end. The ambient music (if switched on) waits until the start screen closes.
- Pictures come from `images/lamp-000-title.jpg`, `images/originals/lamp-clay-unlit.png` and
  `images/originals/video/flame-original.mp4`; rebuild them with `python3 app/tools/make_intro_assets.py`.

## The adventure (pilot)

`www/js/adventure.js` and `www/css/adventure.css`. Finishing a story offers its **story seal**, a short puzzle
built on that story's scripture; breaking it earns a **relic** for the satchel. Relics open **chambers**:
painted scenes you tap through, like Myst.

| Story | Seal | Relic |
|---|---|---|
| Let There Be Light | Light the Days: each round shows what God spoke that day (Genesis 1); drag or tap the matching stone chip into the dial | Stone of Days |
| A Garden Eastward in Eden | The Four Rivers: word search (Genesis 2:10-14) | Onyx Stone |
| Where Art Thou? | The Garden Gate chamber | Coat of Skins |

The Garden Gate: the lamp reveals hidden letters on the stone → spell LIGHT → set the Onyx Stone in the hollow →
the dial of days rises → the Stone of Days unlocks it → turn it to the day of rest → the flaming sword is still →
lay the Coat of Skins on the threshold → the gate opens (Revelation 22:14) → on to Noah.
Progress is saved in `adv` (relics, seals, gate steps) and merged with iCloud like the rest. The Journey screen shows
an Adventure card with the relics, any seals still to break, and the gate. Hints in the adventure cost one drop of oil.
Paintings: `images/originals/adventure/garden-gate/`; rebuild with `python3 app/tools/make_adventure_assets.py`.

## Oil rewards for solving well

- **Story seals** (first time only): +1 oil for **Flawless** (no mistakes, no hints) and +1 for **Swift**
  (Light the Days under 60 s; the Four Rivers under 150 s, up to 2 wrong drags allowed). The Garden Gate:
  +1 for opening it with no hints and no wrong spelling.
- **Word levels:** a level with no wrong words and no hints is **Flawless**; three in a row earn +1 oil
  (shown as three drops on the level-complete card). A mistake or hint starts the count again.

## Tester tools (for Richard while building)

`www/js/dev.js`, on while `TESTER = true`. A **Skip** button floats at the top whenever there is something to get
past: one tap finishes a word level and moves to the next, closes a story card, breaks a story seal, opens the
Garden Gate, or skips the start screen. Skipping never earns oil. **Settings → Tester tools**: Skip button on/off,
unlock all stories, +10 oil, finish Creation/Eden/the Fall, go to the Garden Gate, reset the adventure only, show the
full start screen again.

## Narration

`www/js/narration.js`. The Lamplighter (voice: Oliver Silk, ElevenLabs) reads a story's opening over its painting
before the first level, reads the verse aloud on the story-complete card, and reads what happened next, with
"Coming up" and the next story, when the player moves on. Text is always on screen; **Settings → Narration**
switches the voice off. Background music goes quiet while he speaks. Recorded so far: Creation, Eden, the Fall
and the Garden Gate. Files: `../audio/narration/` (processed) copied to `www/audio/narration/` by `build_www.py`;
originals and the processing command are in `../audio/`.

## Sound

All sounds are original, made in code by `tools/make_sounds.py`, so there is nothing to license.
They use the D major pentatonic scale: harp-like notes for letters (each added letter plays the
next note up, so spelling a word plays a little tune), a harp strum for a word found, bells for
bonus words and the daily verse, a soft low note for a wrong word, a sparkle for hints, a chord
for a finished level and a longer rising shimmer when a story's lamp lights up. Optional ambient
music (D, B minor, G, A, 32 seconds, looping seamlessly) is **off** until switched on in Settings.
On an iPhone the sounds follow the ring/silent switch.

## First build on your Mac

1. Unzip the newest `lamp-apple-vXX.zip` and open `Lamp.xcodeproj`.
2. Click **Lamp** at the top of the file list, then the **Lamp** target, then **Signing & Capabilities**.
   - Team: choose your developer team.
   - Bundle Identifier: `com.richardallinson.Lamp` (already set).
   - Check that **iCloud** is listed with **Key-value storage** ticked. If it isn't, click **+ Capability**, add **iCloud** and tick **Key-value storage**.
3. Choose an iPhone simulator (or your iPhone) at the top and press **Run** (⌘R).
4. To test the purchase in the simulator: **Product > Scheme > Edit Scheme > Run > Options > StoreKit Configuration** should show `Lamp.storekit`. If it shows "None", choose it. Purchases are then free test purchases.

If the build stops with an error, copy the red message into the chat.

## Before App Store submission

- **Turn off the tester tools:** in `www/js/dev.js` set `const TESTER = false;` (removes the Skip button and the Tester tools in Settings).
- In App Store Connect, create a **Non-Consumable** In-App Purchase with Product ID `com.richardallinson.Lamp.fullbible`, price about $4.99, and add it to the 1.0 version.
- App Privacy: **Data Not Collected**.
- The app icon in `Lamp/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon-1024.png` is a placeholder (a gold lamp on navy) and can be replaced.
- The daily verse numbering starts on 1 October 2026 (`dailyNumber` in `www/js/game.js`); change it to the launch date if you prefer #1 on launch day.

## Updating the game

After changing stories or adding pictures:

```
cd content/tools && python3 lamp_levels.py build && cd ../..
python3 app/tools/build_www.py
```
