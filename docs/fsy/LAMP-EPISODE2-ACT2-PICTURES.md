# LAMP Episode 2, Act 2: picture instructions (12 Nov 2026)

Seventeen pictures. Paste the style line first, then one picture's instruction.

**Style line (use with every picture):**
Photorealistic, natural light, Jerusalem Old City in August 2027, warm limestone, no text overlays, no captions, no watermarks, no borders. Any writing in the scene must be too small or too blurred to read.

## Scene pictures (tall, 3:4, 900×1200)

1. **yoel-room-sabbath** (attach the existing Yoel's room picture)
Same room, same table, same window, same five small frames and brass instrument on the table. Sabbath morning: soft bright daylight through the window, the street below empty and quiet, shop shutters closed. A closed book on the table. Nobody in the room.

2. **yoel-room-night** (attach the existing Yoel's room picture)
Same room at night. One desk lamp lit on the table, warm pool of light on four loose pages and a watchmaker's loupe. Window dark blue with three stars. Nobody in the room.

3. **yoel-room-dusk** (attach the existing Yoel's room picture)
Same room in the last light of day, low orange sun coming sideways through the window across the table. A small flat brass case, the size of a paperback, sits on the table beside the brass instrument. Nobody in the room.

4. **chain-shops-night** (attach reference-shops-927.png)
Same narrow covered street and the same watch-repair shop, after dark on Saturday night. The shop's metal shutter is half raised and a lamp is lit inside. The painted clock above the door still shows 9:27. Other shops still shut. Wet-looking stone, one or two people far off.

5. **excavation-ticket**
The entrance to an archaeological park at the south-west corner of the Temple Mount: a small stone ticket booth with a window on the left, a turnstile, a path leading down toward huge Herodian wall stones. Bright morning. A few tourists. View from standing height, path leading forward.

6. **excavation-street**
The paved Herodian street at the foot of the western wall near its south-west corner, seen along the street. Enormous ashlar wall on the left rising out of frame, and high up on it the broken stub of an ancient arch. Large worn paving slabs, a stone kerb running along the right side. Morning light.

7. **fallen-stones**
The same paved street, closer: a heap of huge squared limestone blocks lying where they fell from the wall above, crushing the paving. In front of the heap a single squared stone stands apart, with a niche cut out of one corner large enough for a man to stand in. A low stone kerb runs away from it to the right, made of five separate blocks. No inscription readable.

8. **jaffa-celebration**
Outside Jaffa Gate, bright afternoon, Monday. A happy crowd in the square: people of different nations shaking hands and embracing, flags of many colours with no real country's flag identifiable, a small security booth beside the gate. Festive, peaceful. No politicians, no stage, no banners with words.

9. **jaffa-dusk** (attach reference-jaffa-dusk.png)
Jaffa Gate at sunset, the last sun gold on the top of the wall, the square in blue shadow. Lanterns being lit, a small band playing, people dancing in a ring. A stone ledge in the right foreground, empty. No readable signs.

## The street preacher (2)

10. **jaffa-preacher** (tall, 3:4)
Outside Jaffa Gate on a quiet Sabbath afternoon, few people about. A man in his sixties stands by the wall to the right, plain worn clothes, grey beard, an open Bible held up in one hand, speaking to passers-by who mostly do not stop. He is ordinary and unremarkable; no robes, no halo, no staff, no sign. View from the path leading toward the gate.

11. **preacher** (square portrait, head and shoulders)
The same man, close: weathered face, grey beard, calm direct eyes, plain shirt, the edge of a worn black Bible held against his chest. Stone wall behind, soft daylight. He looks at the camera and is not smiling or frowning.

## People (1)

12. **ticket-student** (square portrait, head and shoulders)
A young Israeli woman in her early twenties working a ticket window, archaeology student, hair tied back, a park staff lanyard, sunglasses pushed up on her head, friendly and slightly bored. Stone booth behind her.

## Objects (square, 960×960, one object, plain dark cloth or wooden table background)

13. **e2-stone**
A weathered squared limestone block seen from above its corner, a deep rectangular niche cut out of one corner. On the top edge a line of worn carved letters, too eroded to read. Museum-photograph lighting.

14. **e2-case**
A small flat brass case the size of a paperback, old and hand-made, closed. On its lid: two small numbered brass wheels, one wheel with a single engraved letter, and a narrow slot with a tiny sliding sun. Engraved lines of small writing too fine to read.

15. **e2-plate**
A thin crescent-shaped brass plate, hand-engraved with a column of fine lines and tiny numerals too small to read, two pin holes at one end. It lies beside a brass ring of the same workmanship, clearly made to fit against it.

16. **e2-pages**
Four typed official pages fanned out on a wooden table, each signed in ink at the bottom, two of them paper-clipped together. A watchmaker's loupe rests on the top page. Text too small to read, no real names, no seals of real countries.

17. **e2-clock**
A small old brass mantel clock, front view. Plain cream face with ONE hand only and no numerals. Three short deep marks on the upper left of the dial before the twelve position. The single hand points just short of the middle mark. No maker's name.
